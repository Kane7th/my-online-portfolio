MySmartRental 2.0

2026

Flagship product: property management software for landlords, tenants, and property managers worldwide. One platform for rent collection, maintenance, messaging, and portfolio reporting. Live on staging at staging.mysmartrental.com (usable today). Replaces spreadsheets and WhatsApp rent chasing with a single dashboard plus native iOS and Android apps for both sides.

Stack: Next.js (App Router, TypeScript, React) for the marketing site, landlord portal, tenant flows, and API routes (/api/health, /api/v1). PostgreSQL for data (health check reports DB reachable). Hosted on nginx / Ubuntu. Multi-locale support and multi-currency UI. Built with modern React patterns, toast notifications, cookie consent, and analytics hooks.

Landlord & tenant features: Sites and units with occupancy tracking; recurring invoices; M-Pesa STK push (Paybill / Till) with SMS receipts; offline payment recording; maintenance tickets with photos and contractor assignment; in-app messaging and broadcast announcements; document storage; finances and reporting; team approvals; multi-currency display (KES, USD, GBP, EUR). Public demo and sign-up funnels without forcing card details for trial units.

What I built: End-to-end product engineering: data model for portfolios and leases, payment reconciliation against units, real-time activity feeds across web and mobile, verified tenant onboarding, and landlord dashboards (properties, tenants, leases, invoices, maintenance, contractors, messages, reports, settings). Payment flows with M-Pesa where operators use it, plus card and bank options, instant payout routing, and arrears visibility. Security-minded defaults (strict transport, frame controls, encrypted sensitive payout fields in UI copy).

Status: Active development on staging; production marketing site at mysmartrental.com. Earlier Flask/React version kept in the timeline below as MySmartRental.com (2025).
